## Standard order terms to adopt (draft, not legal advice)
- Quote valid 7 days. Work starts on a **50% deposit** and written artwork approval.
- Customer is responsible for spelling, logo rights and colour approval on the signed proof.
- Deposit is non-refundable once plates or dies are made.
- Printed quantities may vary ±10%. Faults caused by us are re-made or credited. Customer-approved proofs are not grounds for rejection.
- Balance due before delivery for new accounts, net 15 days for repeat accounts.
- Minimum order value or minimum contribution rule (see `07_product_pricing_model.csv`).
- Cancelled custom orders are charged for completed work and materials.
