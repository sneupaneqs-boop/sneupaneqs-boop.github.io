# First 90 Days: Pilot Plan (Model A)

**Pilot budget cap: NPR 1,50,000 (Config A).** Spend beyond this only after the Day-90 gate. Targets are tied to the Base case in `08_financial_model.xlsx` (new customers 0 / 1 / 2 in months 1–3, billed about NPR 90k in months 2–3). The founder is the responsible person for every task unless stated.

## Prospect-list protocol (days 1–7)
Build a list of **150 or more** businesses from Google Maps, Instagram and Facebook shops, using public information only. Record: name, segment, area, public contact channel, the packaging they use today (photo evidence), and a priority score (see `04_customer_prospect_database.csv`). Mark "contacted" only after contact has actually happened. The base funnel assumed is about 80 contacts a month, about 15% qualified, about half asking for a quote, and a part of those converting.

## Weekly plan

| Week | Focus | Tasks | Target (measurable) | Budget (NPR) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Discovery | Meet CA. Build the prospect list. 5 customer interviews (bakery, skincare, boutique). Test hypotheses on MOQ, price and lead time. | 150 prospects listed; 5 interviews | 5,000 |
| 2 | Supplier verification | Send the RFQ (`06_...md`) to at least 3 suppliers per product. Visit 3–5 workshops. Request samples. | 12 quotes requested; 6 received | 8,000 |
| 3 | Pricing and samples | Load real quotes into the model. Set the price list and minimum order rule. Make 3 sample packs. | Gross margin ≥ 25% at MOQ tiers confirmed | 15,000 |
| 4 | Soft launch | Register and obtain PAN (if CA confirms). Set up Instagram, WhatsApp Business and a one-page catalogue. First 40 outreach messages. | 40 contacts; 5 quote requests | 15,000 |
| 5–6 | Outreach | 25 contacts a week. Hand over sample kits to the best 10 prospects. Quote within 24 hours. | 50 contacts; 8 quotes; **first paid order with 50% deposit** | 15,000 |
| 7–8 | Fulfil and learn | Produce the first orders through suppliers with QC checks. Document lead times and defects. Ask for referrals and photos. | 2 paid orders; on-time delivery ≥ 90%; zero unresolved quality complaints | 10,000 |
| 9–10 | Expand | Outreach 25 a week. Offer reorders. Start the referral offer (5% credit on the referrer's next order). | 3 paying customers cumulative; billed ≥ NPR 60,000 | 12,000 |
| 11–12 | Scale test | Test one corporate-gifting lead. Test recurring supply plans. | 4 paying customers; billed ≥ NPR 90,000; collections ≥ the 50% deposits | 12,000 |
| 13 | **Gate review** | Compute contribution per order, conversion, cash. Decide continue, change or stop. | See gate below | 3,000 |

Total planned pilot spend: about NPR 95,000 on weekly items, plus the NPR 72,000 Config A set-up lines, which are mostly consumed in the first weeks. Keep the total at or under NPR 1,50,000 including the working-capital reserve of NPR 60,000.

## Acquisition tactics
- **Pitch (small brands):** "Branded packaging in your own size and logo, from 100 pieces, with a free paper sample before you pay for print. One person handles design, print and delivery."
- **Instagram/WhatsApp opener:** "Hi [name], I'm [founder]. I help [bakeries/skincare brands] get branded boxes from small runs. Can I send a sample of a [box type] sized for [their product]?"
- **Email:** Use the same text. Add 2 photos of samples and a price range table.
- **Sample kit:** 3 small samples (bag, carton, sticker) plus a one-page price sheet. Cost about NPR 800–1,200 per kit.
- **Referral:** 5% credit on the next order for both sides.
- **Retention:** Calendar a reorder reminder at 60 and 90 days. Hold the die and artwork file so reorders take one message.
- **Minimum order and deposit policy:** 50% deposit. Orders with contribution below NPR 3,000 are re-quoted at the next quantity or refused.
- **Customer acquisition cost (assumption):** about NPR 10,000/month marketing + about NPR 12,000/month of the founder's sampling effort ÷ about 3 new customers = about NPR 7,000 per customer. At a contribution of about NPR 5,300 per order, each customer needs about 1.3 orders to repay acquisition. (Low confidence.)

## Day-90 gate: continue, change or stop

| Result at day 90 | Decision |
|---|---|
| ≥ 4 paying customers, billed ≥ NPR 90,000, contribution/order ≥ NPR 3,000, quotes confirm gross margin ≥ 25%, collections on time | **Continue.** Release the rest of the budget up to NPR 5,00,000. Founder pay may start only when monthly operating profit is positive for 2 months. |
| 2–3 paying customers, or margins 15–25% | **Change.** Narrow to the best segment (likely skincare/candle/soap brands), raise prices or minimums, change suppliers, re-test for 60 days. Cap further spend at NPR 50,000. |
| < 2 paying customers, or billed < NPR 50,000, or gross margin < 15% | **Stop.** Do not buy anything more. Recover stock and samples. Total loss capped at about NPR 1.5 lakh or less. |

## Stop-loss rules during the pilot
- Pause all spending if, for 3 consecutive weeks from week 5, there are no new quote requests.
- Pause if two orders in a row are below the minimum contribution.
- Do not extend credit before a customer has paid on time twice.
- Total spend never exceeds NPR 1,50,000 before the gate.
