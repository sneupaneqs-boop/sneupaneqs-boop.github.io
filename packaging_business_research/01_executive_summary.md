## Decision (read this first)

**CONDITIONAL GO, as a staged pilot. Do not spend the full NPR 5,00,000 up front, and do not buy machinery.**

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Feasible within NPR 5,00,000? | Only as a design-and-sourcing business with no powered machinery. A workshop does not fit. A single label cutter costs about NPR 1.84–3.04 lakh before freight and duty (advertised Indian prices), and unverified landed cost pushes it to about NPR 2.3–3.8 lakh. |
| Model to select | **Model A, design and sourcing agency** (weighted score 3.85/5). Fallback: **Model B, hybrid studio** (3.18/5), only after a pilot gate. Model C, in-house workshop, scores 1.86/5. |
| Launch products | Printed corrugated mailer/bakery boxes, branded kraft paper bags, folding cartons (skincare, soap, candle brands), rigid gift boxes (a few high-ticket orders), and stickers/labels as an add-on. |
| Outsource | All printing, die-cutting, lamination, gluing and rigid-box making. Keep design, prototyping by hand, sampling, quoting, supplier QC, delivery and invoicing in-house. |
| Machines to buy | **None** at launch. Hand tools only (about NPR 30,000). First machine to consider after the gate is a sticker/label cutter, funded from profit. |
| Working capital to reserve | NPR 1,85,000, plus NPR 50,000 contingency. |
| Break-even (Base) | Fixed cost NPR 32,500/month. Break-even sales about **NPR 1.73 lakh/month, or 6 orders/month**, before founder pay. With NPR 15,000/month founder pay: about NPR 2.53 lakh/month, or 9 orders. Operating break-even in **month 7** before founder pay, month 9 after. |
| Peak cash need | About NPR 4.75 lakh with an unpaid founder (Base), and about NPR 5.38 lakh if founder pay starts in month 7. |
| Maximum to commit before demand is proven | **NPR 1,50,000** (Config A). |

## What the numbers say (Base case, 12 months)

- Revenue is NPR 20.97 lakh from 44 new customers and 74 orders. Contribution is NPR 3.94 lakh.
- Gross margin at each product's minimum-viable tier is **24.8%**, and the contribution-margin ratio is **18.8%**. This is thin.
- If the founder takes **no pay**, the Base case stays solvent. The lowest cash is NPR 24,736 in month 8, and ending cash is NPR 57,652.
- If founder pay of NPR 15,000/month starts in month 7, cash runs **negative from month 8** and bottoms at −NPR 37,735. The 5-lakh ceiling is breached.
- Downside case (half the new customers, 40% lower repeat, +10% supplier cost, +30 collection days): **not solvent**, with a cash shortfall of about NPR 1.9–2.8 lakh.
- Upside case (+40% customers, +30% repeat): solvent, minimum cash +NPR 45,995 with founder pay, profit about +NPR 2.1 lakh.
- Stress tests with an unpaid founder: only "repeat purchases 50% lower" stays solvent. Price −10%, supplier cost +10% or +15%, acquisition 50% slower, and 30-day-later payments all breach zero cash. Supplier failure, seasonality and write-offs breach it mildly (−NPR 20–25k).

**The business is viable only if the founder can live without a salary for about 9 months, supplier quotes confirm margins near 25% or better, and sales run at or above the Base ramp. The cushion is thin.**

## What could make it fail

1. Supplier prices come in higher than assumed. Every supplier cost is an assumption because no quotation was obtained.
2. Customers keep using established box makers and printers, or order only 100–250 units.
3. Slow customer acquisition, which is the single biggest sensitivity.
4. Customers pay late while suppliers need money up front.
5. Quality or rework problems with third-party printers.

## What must be proven before committing more than NPR 1,50,000

1. **At least 3 written supplier quotations per product** (see `06_supplier_quotation_request.md`) confirming a gross margin of at least 25% at the minimum order sizes.
2. **At least 3 paying customers (with deposits) within 90 days**, and at least NPR 90,000 billed.
3. Confirmation of the VAT, PAN and registration route with a Nepal CA.

## Honest limits of this research

- Web research used a US-only search tool and page fetches. No supplier, customer or machine dealer was contacted, and **no quotations were received**.
- Only one competitor's price list (The Box House Nepal) was directly fetched. Other competitor details are search-snippet level.
- No market size or count of Kathmandu businesses could be verified. The prospect database lists segments, not named businesses.
- The skill repository requested (`EdisonChenAl/agent-reach`) could not be cloned because it asked for credentials, so it was not used.
- Subagents were not used. The workstreams were run sequentially in one session.
- Legal items are from secondary sources and must be confirmed with a Nepal CA or lawyer.
